# Tram departure clock

clockESP32 status: design · V2 · 2026-11-10

HOLO

←→ rotate · Tram departure clock (simplified model)

A wall device (318 × 160 × 80 mm) for Rostock, stop Kabutzenhof. The front carries a 7" display with real-time departures from the VMV-EFA API (stop de:13003:804, lines 1 and 5, refresh every 30 s, red "+N" for delays, cancelled trips struck through). The back carries a quartz analogue clock. The housing has three printed parts and no glue: a body with ribs as tongue and groove for display and dial, a wall block with dovetail and detent bumps, and a wall plate (two countersunk 4 mm screws). Slide it on from above, pull it off upwards. Status: design only. Nothing printed yet, firmware untested, board dimensions still to be measured on the delivered unit. Any EFA city can be ported.
