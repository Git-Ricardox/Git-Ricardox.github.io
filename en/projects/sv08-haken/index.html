# SV08 coat hook

print3D-Druck status: planned · V1 · 2026-11-10

HOLO

←→ rotate · Sovol SV08 (simplified model)

My coat hook wobbles, and one day a jacket will end up on the floor. The sane fix costs three euros at the hardware store. Instead, I calibrate the whole machine first: a Sovol SV08 with Eddy sensor, Klipper mainline + eddy-ng, sliced in OrcaSlicer. Temperature, flow, pressure advance, retraction and input shaper get measured and written down, then one hook is printed. Status: the printer is calibrated and documented; the hook itself is not designed or printed yet. Numbers that depend on the hook (print time, grams, number of test prints) are filled in after printing, never guessed.

## build-log

2026-11-10
Benchy edges and bridges look wrong. Root cause found: the front part-cooling fan does not spin (rear one does).
2026-11-11
Belts retensioned, input shaper redone: X MZV 62.8 Hz, Y MZV 43.8 Hz. Pressure advance 0.040, PLA flow 0.93.
2026-11-11
Extruder will not load: PLA was loaded with a cold nozzle (163 °C) and jammed. Fix: heat to 230 °C, extrude slowly. Rule: never load PLA below about 220 °C.
